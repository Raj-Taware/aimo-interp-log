## What changes our plan

### 1. Early-reasoning correctness probes (biggest lever)

- Qwen3-8B (reasoning mode), 1,500 MATH problems: PCA(<=128) + logistic
  regression on final-layer hidden states pooled over the last 4 tokens
  predicts final-answer correctness with **AUC ~0.84 after only 4 reasoning
  tokens**, ~0.8 up to ~100 tokens; entropy+length baseline best 0.59.
  [arXiv 2511.14773, 3-0]
- Question-only "in-advance correctness direction" (one forward pass) works
  on trivia but **falters on math** — needs math-specific labels.
  [2509.10625, 3-0]
- Probe on reasoning-model hidden states verifies intermediate answers;
  early exit at threshold 0.85 cut tokens ~24% at unchanged accuracy.
  [2504.05419, 3-0; abstract checked by hand]

**Use.** Robustness labels are scarce (34), but T-015 will produce ~7,000
graded generations (correct / incorrect vs T-006 answers). Train a per-model
early-correctness probe on those; at test time generate ~16 reasoning tokens
for the original and each edit; **robustness score = predicted-correctness
drop under the edits.** This is Phase 2 (look at the solution, not the
reading), made cheap and trainable.

### 2. Head-level distraction (refines H1)

- Qwen3-4B-Thinking on misleading-math (arithmetic + irrelevant sentences):
  incorrectly answered samples put far more strong attention on distractor
  tokens. Independent support for H1's direction in a Qwen-family thinking
  model. [2601.07226, 3-0]
- Distractor effects run through a **small set of heads**: 3.2-10.7% of
  heads drive "contextual entrainment" in Llama-3.1-8B; zeroing them drops
  distractor-token ranks from 2nd/4th to 53rd/68th. [2505.09338, 3-0]
- A cheap per-head "contextual score" (attention mass on a span, averaged
  over tokens) finds such heads: 2/672 heads > 0.2 in Llama-3.2-3B.
  [2503.23306, 3-0]
- Lookback Lens: per-head attention-ratio features + logistic regression
  match or beat hidden-state probes out of domain (AUROC 66 vs 56-59).
  Code public. [2407.07071, 3-0 / 2-1]

**Use.** Keep excess attention **per head** instead of averaging over heads
and layers. Select "distractor heads" **without labels** (heads with the
highest mean excess attention across unlabelled problems), score = mean over
those heads. Pre-register before T-015 labels land.

### 3. Lower priority

- Reasoning-vs-memorization linear direction (difference of means, one pass;
  Spearman 0.84 with GPT-4o reasoning intensity) [2503.23084, 3-0] — but a
  TF-IDF classifier gets AUROC 0.99 on the same split, so it tracks domain
  and vocabulary [2608.21919, 3-0]. Unlikely to separate within olympiad math.
- Contamination probes (DICE [2406.04197], probe attack [2406.01333]) need a
  contaminated/clean model pair or proxy fine-tuning — impractical here.
- Qwen-Scope: open SAEs for 7 Qwen3/Qwen3.5 variants [2605.11887; variant
  list not confirmed]. Useful for the write-up (name the features behind
  distractor heads), not for the leaderboard.
