### Where the Skywork failure sits

- 9 of the 12 differing cells are last-bit float32 differences in
  `distract_attnx_d*` (rel ~6e-8), i.e. the expected reduction-order effect.
- The other 3 are **`distract_attnx_mean` on all 3 rows: NaN in T-013, finite
  now** (0.5353, 0.4843, 0.4926). That is what flips `nan_positions_equal`
  and gives the infinite relative diff.
- The pooled `profile__distract__attnx` arrays are equal to print precision
  and both have NaN at the first and last depth for Skywork (DeepSeek and
  Qwen profiles have no NaN, which is why they pass). So T-013 computed the
  mean as NaN when the profile has NaN endpoints, and the new code returns a
  finite mean.
- Hunch (not verified): the new per-head path in `geometry._attention_excess`
  or the feature code now takes a NaN-ignoring mean (or derives the mean from
  the head matrix), where T-013 used a plain mean. All non-attnx features are
  bit-exact.
- Per-head matrices were written: DeepSeek (10, 36, 32), Qwen (14, 8, 16),
  Skywork (3, 28, 28) with 0.13% NaN cells; `_case` rows match.
