**Why.** Adversarial review of v4: it compares unaligned summaries with raw
cosine before any reasoning, so it mostly measured problem length.
`self-perturb/src/selfperturb/geometry.py` replaces that with token-aligned
representational geometry (CKA, whitened cosine, answer-position cosine,
logit-lens KL, distractor excess-attention), per layer. This task produces
the data; Claude runs the pre-registered evaluation below.

## Pre-registered evaluation (Claude, fixed before any data)

Data: MATH = 137 DeepSeek-labelled problems; TV2 = 34 labelled train-main-v2
rows (20 problems). TV2 significance by **problem-level** label permutation.

- **H1 mechanism, excess attention** (no fitting): `distract_attnx_mean`,
  higher = spurious. Pass: AUC(spurious) > 0.60 on TV2 with p < 0.05, and
  in the same direction on MATH after adding length (`L + attnx` beats `L`
  per the T-004 rule).
- **H2 mechanism, mid-depth geometry** (no fitting): mean of
  `distract_cka_d0.4/0.55/0.7`, lower = spurious. Same pass rule as H1.
- **H3 classifier**: the v4 procedure (SelectKBest + balanced LR, inner CV)
  trained on MATH geometry features, per-model standardization using each
  model's own TV2 batch (label-free), per-batch median decision on TV2.
  Pass: TV2 AUC > 0.675 (v4) with p < 0.05.
- Any pass on TV2 only, failing MATH-beyond-length, is reported as
  "in-distribution signal, mechanism unconfirmed".

## Review (Claude, 2026-10-08): done — pre-registered results

Data verified as reported (canary and determinism bitwise exact). Evaluation
run as pre-registered on the data as dumped (`results/grok/T-012/eval_preregistered.json`):

| hypothesis | TV2 (34 rows, 20 problems) | MATH beyond length | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 excess attention | AUC 0.790, problem-level p = 0.019 | +0.015, 15/25 wins (bar +0.03) | **TV2-only: in-distribution signal, mechanism unconfirmed** |
| H2 mid-depth CKA | AUC 0.629, p = 0.160 | +0.002 | fail |
| H3 MATH-trained classifier | AUC 0.463, p = 0.583 | — | fail |

Three hypotheses tested: Bonferroni p for H1 ~0.057. Exploratory (not
pre-registered): per-model H1 AUC Qwen 0.91, Olmo 0.93, DeepSeek 0.62
(Skywork n=3); by depth the signal sits at the shallowest (0.10: 0.96) and
deepest (0.85: 0.85) attention layers, near chance mid-depth. On MATH the
direction REVERSES (AUC 0.37) — the two label sets may reflect different
failure types; do not claim a general mechanism yet. As a batch-median
decision rule H1 gives balanced 0.642 on TV2 (v4 0.692) because forcing 50%
spurious costs on a 72%-robust set; ranking (AUC) is where it leads.

Grok's two observations were real bugs, fixed in geometry.py (hybrid
attention layer mapping via `config.layer_types`; non-finite token states
dropped per layer). Neither touched H1/H2 inputs. Re-run in T-013.
